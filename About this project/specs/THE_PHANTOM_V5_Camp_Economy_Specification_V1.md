# THE PHANTOM V5 Camp Economy Specification V1

THE PHANTOM V5 — Camp Economy Specification (V1)
Camp Philosophy: Camps are long-term civilizations. Squads compete in Sessions; Camps compete for history and Legacy Wars.
Camp Eligibility
Reach Camp Master milestone.
Complete Camp Master promotion requirements.
Spend required Legacy Tokens.
Choose Camp Name.
Create Camp Motto.
Select an official Camp Emblem from a curated library (no custom uploads).
Camp colors/animations are tied to the selected emblem.
Official Emblems
Launch with ~60 official 3D emblems.
Examples: Lion, Wolf, Phoenix, Dragon, Robot, Bolt, Crown, Titan.
Camp and Squad logos must come from the official library.
Only user profile pictures allow custom uploads.
Official emblems enable premium animations and seasonal effects.
Treasury
Camp Reward Pool: 5% of every Official Session.
Split: 80% Camp Treasury / 20% Squad Treasury.
Eligible leaders may withdraw up to 10% each season after governance checks.
Projects
Members donate Tokens to Projects that unlock shared Camp bonuses.
Legacy War
5% of every Official Session also funds the Legacy War Reserve, contested every 90 days.
