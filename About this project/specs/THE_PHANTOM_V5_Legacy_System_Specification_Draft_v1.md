# THE PHANTOM V5 Legacy System Specification Draft v1

THE PHANTOM V5 — LEGACY SYSTEM SPECIFICATION (Draft v1)
Purpose
The Legacy System is the permanent progression system of THE PHANTOM. Sessions, rewards and money are temporary; Legacy is permanent. Every meaningful action contributes toward building a player's Legacy.
Core Philosophy
'Enter the Phantom. Build a Legacy.' The game mechanics exist to serve the player's long-term Legacy.
Three Economies
1. Session Economy: session tokens and gameplay items.2. Legacy Economy: Legacy, Legacy Influence, Kata progression, titles, reputation and privileges.3. Financial Economy: entry fees, prize pools, purchases and camp funding. Money must never directly buy Legacy.
What is Legacy?
A player's permanent identity, history and reputation inside THE PHANTOM, including achievements, seasons, squads, camps, rivalries and milestones.
What is Legacy Influence?
The measurable strength of a player's Legacy. It is earned through participation, leadership, teamwork and consistency. It cannot be bought, transferred or traded.
How Legacy Influence is Earned
Complete sessions, win championships, finish highly, revive teammates, lead squads, contribute to camps, recruit active players, complete seasonal objectives, remain consistent, create valuable community content and participate in official events.
Principles
Legacy Influence cannot be purchased, transferred or traded. It compounds over time, rewards consistency, rewards contribution and has no final endpoint.
Legacy Progression Loop
Participate in Sessions → Earn Rewards → Gain Legacy Influence → Increase Kata → Unlock Privileges → Join Bigger Competitions → Lead the Community → Strengthen Legacy → Repeat.
Kata Progression
Traditional levels are replaced with Kata. Each Kata contains multiple internal ranks before promotion, creating constant visible progression.
Legacy Privileges
Higher Kata unlocks opportunities such as creating squads, switching or creating camps, elite sessions, exclusive events, creator verification, ambassador opportunities, community voting and prestige identities.
Legacy Profile
Every player owns a permanent Legacy Profile showing their story: first session, greatest victories, streaks, rivalries, revives, camp history, championships and rare titles.
Seasons
Seasonal rankings reset, Session Tokens disappear, but Legacy remains and continues to grow.
Design Rule
Every new feature must answer: 'How does this strengthen the player's Legacy?'
