# THE PHANTOM V5 Legacy War Specification V1

THE PHANTOM V5 — Legacy War Specification (V1)
Philosophy
Legacy War is the pinnacle event of THE PHANTOM. Official Sessions build Squads, Seasons build Camps, and Legacy War determines the dominant Camp of the world.
War Cycle
Occurs every 3 Seasons (90 days).
Every Official Session contributes 5% of its prize pool to the Legacy War Reserve.
The growing reserve is visible to all players throughout the season.
Qualification
All Camps qualify.
Season performance determines seeding and starting position.
Structure
Duration: 7 days.
Phase 1 – Mobilization (2 days): projects, recruitment, strategy and preparation.
Phase 2 – War Campaign (4 days): one Official War Session per day with cumulative War Points.
Phase 3 – Final Stand (1 day): decisive championship session with increased score weighting.

Projects & Alliances
Camp Projects funded during the season become active during Legacy War.
Temporary alliances are permitted.
Camp Leaders may publicly break alliances once during the war.

Reward Distribution
Champion Camp: 50% of Legacy War Reserve.
Second Place: 20%.
Third Place: 10%.
Remaining Qualified Camps share 20%.
Within each Camp, rewards are distributed according to player contribution.
Champion Rewards
World Champion title until next Legacy War.
Golden Camp Name.
Exclusive animated Camp emblem.
Champion Banner across the application.
Golden profile aura for all Camp members.
Historic entry into the Legacy Hall.
No gameplay advantage—prestige only.
Leadership Succession
If a Camp Master is inactive for 14 consecutive days, the Camp enters Leadership at Risk.
Eligible Camp Masters may challenge to assume leadership.
The Camp retains its history, treasury, members and identity.
