# THE PHANTOM V5 Squad Economy Specification V1

THE PHANTOM V5 — Squad Economy Specification (V1)
Philosophy
A Squad is the player's family inside THE PHANTOM. Players build friendships, compete together, develop reputation, and represent their Camp through their Squad.
Squad Creation
Unlocked at the Squad Master milestone.
Player must complete all Squad Master promotion requirements.
Pay the Squad Creation Token cost.
Choose Squad Name and Squad Motto.
Select an Official Squad Emblem from the curated 3D emblem library (no custom uploads).
Squad Capacity
Maximum members: 15 players.
Every member matters and inactive players are noticeable.
Recruitment
Open Recruitment.
Request to Join.
Invite Only.
Activity Rule
Members inactive for more than 7 consecutive days are automatically removed to keep squads active.
Squad Roles
Squad Master: full management permissions.
Deputy: assists with moderation and recruitment.
Officer: recruitment and coordination.
Member: standard participation.
Squad Treasury
Receives its allocation from the Session Economy.
Funds Squad Projects and seasonal development.
Eligible Squad Masters may withdraw approved treasury rewards after governance checks.
Squad Projects
Energy upgrades.
Gameplay bonuses.
Recruitment boosts.
Identity upgrades.
Members contribute Tokens until projects are completed.
Squad Identity
Name.
Motto.
Official Emblem.
Animated Banner.
Historic statistics.
Squad Reputation
Historic wins.
Current rank.
Win streak.
Camp contribution.
Legacy War participation.
Squad Chat
Every Squad has a private real-time Squad Chat.
Only current Squad members can access it.
Supports text messages and emoji reactions.
System messages announce joins, removals, promotions, completed projects and upcoming sessions.
Squad Master and Deputies can pin important messages.
Players immediately lose access to Squad Chat after leaving or being removed from the Squad.
Design Intent
The Squad should become the strongest emotional attachment for a player. Leaving a Squad should feel like leaving a family, creating long-term retention and social commitment.
