# THE PHANTOM V5 Creator and Social Economy Specification V1

THE PHANTOM V5Creator & Social Economy Specification V1
Purpose
The Creator Economy transforms every player into a potential ambassador for THE PHANTOM. Every player can share gameplay externally while advanced players unlock the in-app creator ecosystem.
1. Public Session Recording
All players can record gameplay, voice and optional facecam, download clips, and share to WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Telegram and X.
2. Automatic Branding
Every exported video includes THE PHANTOM watermark, player name, squad, camp, Legacy Influence, referral link and optional QR code.
3. Automatic Ending Card
Every video ends with a branded 2–3 second outro showing player identity and a Join The Battle message.
4. Creator Platform (Strategist)
Strategists unlock publishing, followers, comments, reactions, shares, livestreaming, creator analytics, community posts and trending.
5. Creator Analytics
Views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, followers, referral installs, engagement rate and Legacy Influence earned.
6. Creator Influence Points
Like=1 CIP, Comment=3 CIP, Share=5 CIP, 90% Watch=2 CIP, Referral=25 CIP. Every 100 CIP awards +10 Legacy Influence.
7. Legacy Influence Rule
Legacy Influence cannot be purchased. It is earned through gameplay, leadership, community contribution and creator activity.
8. Referral Rewards
Successful referral: 500 Tokens and +5 Referral Score. If the referred player reaches Pathfinder Stage 5, the referrer earns 1 Legacy Coin.
9. Referral Milestones
5: Profile Frame; 10: 1 Legacy Coin; 25: Emote Pack; 50: Animated Effect; 100: Recruiter Badge; 250: Banner Theme; 500: Legendary Recruiter Title.
10. Internal Feed Eligibility
Only Strategists and above can publish inside THE PHANTOM. Everyone else may still record, download and share externally.
11. Social Feed Philosophy
Feed prioritizes followed players, squad members, camp members, trending creators and official announcements.
12. Design Philosophy
Gameplay creates content, content attracts players, and new players strengthen the Legacy economy.
