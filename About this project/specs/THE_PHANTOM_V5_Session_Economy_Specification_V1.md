# THE PHANTOM V5 Session Economy Specification V1

THE PHANTOM V5 — Session Economy Specification (V1)
Purpose: Define how Tokens are generated, distributed and consumed during Official and Private Sessions.
1. Official Session Structure
6 Official Sessions every day (one every 4 hours).
Minimum Session Length: 20 minutes.
Average Spin Duration: 6 seconds.
Theoretical Maximum Spins: 200.
Expected Average Spins: 160 (allowing time for strategy, targeting and item usage).
2. Token Generation During Gameplay
Wheel outcomes follow the existing project specification (Advance, Acquire, Discover, Steal and Void).
Target gameplay earnings per session: 850–1,050 Tokens.

4. Expected Total Earnings
Average total per Official Session: 1,050–1,350 Tokens.
Average total for completing all 6 Official Sessions: 6,300–8,100 Tokens.
5. Private Sessions
Can be created by Players, Squads or Camps.
Entry options: Free, Token Entry or Real-Money Entry.
Can award custom prizes defined by the host.
Do not replace Official Sessions for core progression.

8. Rewarded Ads
10 rewarded ads available per cycle.
50 Tokens per ad.
Maximum of 500 Tokens per cycle.
60-minute cooldown before the next batch becomes available.
